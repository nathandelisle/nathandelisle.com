Geometric Uncertainty for Outlier Detection

Ask a model the same question thirty times, and look at the shape its answers make in semantic space.

When it knows, the answers have low volume. When it hallucinates, they scatter into a cloud with greater volume.

This flags unreliable answers with no ground truth needed — but it fails for confident, consistent hallucinations.
