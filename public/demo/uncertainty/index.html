Geometric Uncertainty for Outlier Detection

Ask a model the same question thirty times, and look at the shape its answers make in semantic space.

When the model knows the answer, the answers have low volume.

When the model hallucinates, the answers have high volume.

This flags traditional hallucinations, but fails for confident hallucinations.
